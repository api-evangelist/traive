# Traive

> Traive is a Brazilian agri-fintech that connects agribusiness with financial investors, using AI and data analytics to simplify credit access in the agricultural sector. Traive Agro handles agricultural credit commercialization (risk analysis, process automation, financing access); Traive Finance provides credit risk analysis and access to qualified agricultural assets. 160K+ registered producers; R$6B+ in structured recurring credit operations.

Traive publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or developer documentation. Its platform authenticates through a Keycloak OpenID Connect provider (realm traive-prod) whose public discovery metadata names an internal service estate (credit, farmers, retailers, documents, ERP integration, GraphQL).
